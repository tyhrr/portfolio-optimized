## Platform

## Users

People evaluating Alan Salva for a hybrid technical role: hiring managers and recruiters at SaaS companies looking for someone who sits between customers and the development team (support engineer, implementation specialist, technical support with coding ability), plus developers on those teams who check his code. They arrive from a CV, LinkedIn or a direct link, usually on a laptop, sometimes on a phone, and decide within a minute whether to read on or get in touch.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio that shows Alan is both a capable support professional and a developer who builds real tools in Python and JavaScript. Success: a visitor understands the hybrid profile in the first screen, sees working projects, and has an obvious way to contact him or download his CV.
